# VERIQ

## Organizational Truth & Intelligence Engine

> Know the company. Understand the money. Detect the risk. Find what others miss.

**Verify. Understand. Predict.**

VERIQ 2.0 is an evidence-first organisational intelligence platform. Give it any organisation, connect authorised data, interrogate identity, documents, money and relationships, surface inconsistencies, quantify financial condition **only from evidence**, and monitor what changes.

It is one core engine plus sector audit packs (SME first; SACCO, MFI, NGO, school, hospital, church, professional, logistics, fintech as catalogs). It is not KYB, not GRC, not an auditor, and not a credit bureau.

AI explains. Deterministic engines write facts. Unverified inference never becomes a fact.

The original corporate-risk spine (scan, radar, graph, score) remains the product path. 2.0 extends it with an evidence graph, authorised document intelligence, and contradiction/financial engines. See [`BUILD.md`](./BUILD.md) §0 for build order.

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

VERIQ is an AI-powered Corporate Risk Intelligence platform that continuously evaluates an organisation's technology, code, infrastructure, data, people, vendors, regulatory obligations, financial dependencies, AI usage and external environment.

Unlike traditional GRC platforms that primarily ask whether a company is compliant with predefined controls, VERIQ continuously asks:

- What is this company?
- What systems does it depend on?
- What data does it handle?
- What could go wrong?
- What has changed?
- What external events could affect it?
- Which risks are becoming more serious?
- What evidence supports the risk?
- What should management do next?
- What happens if management does nothing?

VERIQ creates a continuously evolving digital representation of the organisation called the **VERIQ Company Model**.

This model becomes the foundation for:

- Risk detection
- Risk scoring
- Regulatory intelligence
- Cybersecurity intelligence
- Third-party risk
- Operational risk
- Technology risk
- AI governance
- Business resilience
- Scenario simulation
- Executive reporting
- Board reporting
- Investor due diligence
- Bank/insurance risk assessment

- Legal uses another system.
